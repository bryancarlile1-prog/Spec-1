# Retailer outreach script

Use the phone first and follow up by email. Fill in the **[brackets]** with real details. The script follows the board's advice: sell before you buy, be honest that you're brokering, and ask for a written commitment.

**Rule for every call:** Never say you hold goods you don't control. You're offering to *source* a specific load for them.

## 1. Opening (20 seconds)
> "Hi, is this **[Name]**? I'm **[Your name]** with **[Company]**. I help independent retailers source **[category, e.g. household goods and seasonal items]** at closeout prices. I'm not selling anything today. I'm trying to learn how you buy, and I have a couple of real offers to test. Do you have 5 minutes?"

If no: *"No problem. When's a better time this week?"*

## 2. Discovery questions (listen more than you talk)
1. "What categories do you buy on closeout or liquidation today?"
2. "Where do you get it from now: brokers, auctions, reps?"
3. "What size do you buy: single pallets, half trucks, full loads?"
4. "What price range makes a deal worth it? For example, what % of retail?"
5. "How far will you have it shipped?"
6. "What's gone wrong on past buys?" *(Let them vent. This is your trust roadmap.)*
7. "How fast do you need to say yes when a good lot shows up?"

## 3. The offer
> "Here's what I can do. When I find a lot that fits what you just described, I send you the **manifest, real photos, condition, and the all-in delivered price** before you commit. You'd pay through **[protected payment / on delivery]**, and if it doesn't match what I showed you, you get **[refund / credit]**. I'd charge **[5–10]%** on top of the goods. There's no subscription and no fee to see deals."

Then give a **specific example** from your software, but only after you've confirmed it by phone:
> "For example, I'm looking at **[X units of Y, condition, location]** at about **[Z¢ on the dollar]**, delivered around **[$]**. Would something like that interest you?"

## 4. The ask (this is the point of the call)
Pick one, easiest first:
- **Best:** "Would you sign a short letter saying you'd buy **[a pallet of X]** at **[price]** if I deliver it as described?"
- **Good:** "Would you put down a small refundable deposit to hold the first lot?"
- **Minimum:** "Can I send you deals that match your list, and can I check back in a week?"

## 5. Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I already have brokers." | "That's fine. I'm not asking you to switch. Try one pallet and compare it to your usual source." |
| "I've been burned before." | "That's why you see photos, a manifest and the condition first. What went wrong last time?" |
| "Do you own the goods?" | "I source the load and match it to you. You won't pay until the load is confirmed and there's a guarantee." *(Only say this if it's true.)* |
| "Too far / freight kills the margin." | "You'd see the delivered price up front. If the freight makes it a bad deal, I won't send it." |
| "Send me info." | "Happy to. What categories and price range should I match? I'll send a real example, not a brochure." |
| "Not interested." | "Understood. Is it a timing issue, or do you just not buy this way?" *(Note the answer, then thank them.)* |

## 6. Close
> "Thanks, **[Name]**. To confirm: you're looking for **[category, size, price range, region]**. I'll send you a real example by **[day]**. Can I call you then?"

## 7. Follow-up email (send within an hour)
> **Subject:** Following up: [category] lots for [Store name]
>
> Hi **[Name]**,
>
> Thanks for the time today. You mentioned you buy **[category]** in **[size]** at around **[% of retail]**, delivered within **[distance]**.
>
> I'll send you a real example by **[day]**, with photos, a manifest, condition and the delivered price up front.
>
> If you'd like me to hold the first lot for you, a short one-line letter saying you'd buy **[X]** at **[price]** is all I need.
>
> **[Your name] · [Phone] · [Company]**

## 8. Call log
Copy this into a spreadsheet, one row per call.

| Store | Contact | Category | Size/price wanted | Region | Past problem | Ask made | Result (LOI / deposit / maybe / no) | Next step + date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## What to measure
- Calls made (target **20** in one category and region)
- Number who agreed to talk (rate)
- **Written commitments or deposits** (target **3–5**). This is the number the board cares about.
- Most common objection and most common past problem

## Before you use it
- **The guarantee and payment terms** must be ones you can actually honor. Don't promise a refund or protected payment you can't back.
- **Legal and brand rules:** Check any resale restrictions on the goods before you offer them.
- **Category and region:** Pick one of each before you start. The script works best when it's specific.
