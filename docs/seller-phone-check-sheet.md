# Seller phone-check sheet

Use this to verify a load your software found **before** you promise it to any buyer. Fill out one sheet per load. The goal is to find out whether the lead is real, available, cleanly owned and profitable.

**Rule:** Don't quote a load to a retailer until every "must pass" item below is confirmed by a person, not just by the software.

## 1. Opening (15 seconds)
> "Hi, this is **[Your name]** with **[Company]**. I saw your listing for **[description]** and I'm calling to confirm it's still available. Do you have two minutes?"

## 2. Availability (must pass)
- [ ] Is this load still available today? *(Y / N)*
- [ ] Has anyone else committed to it or put a hold on it? *(Y / N)*
- [ ] How long will you hold it for me, and at what price? *(hours / days)*
- [ ] Are you the owner, or are you selling on someone's behalf? *(Owner / Broker / Carrier / Other)*

## 3. Ownership and title (must pass)
- [ ] Who holds title to the goods right now? *(Name)*
- [ ] Can you give me a bill of sale or written proof of ownership? *(Y / N)*
- [ ] Is there any lien, claim or hold from a carrier, shipper, insurer or bank? *(Y / N, details)*
- [ ] If it's unclaimed or refused freight, has the legal notice or waiting period been completed? *(Y / N / N/A)*
- [ ] Who is responsible if the goods are recalled or the title is disputed? *(Name)*

## 4. What the goods are (must pass)
- [ ] Is there an itemized manifest (SKUs, quantities)? *(Y / N, and I asked for a copy)*
- [ ] Brand, product type, units per pallet, total pallets *(fill in)*
- [ ] Condition: new / open box / customer returns / damaged / expired *(circle, then write detail)*
- [ ] Expiry or best-by dates, if applicable *(fill in)*
- [ ] Any recalled, counterfeit-risk, or regulated items (food, cosmetics, electronics, batteries)? *(Y / N)*
- [ ] Any brand resale limits (no discount, no online, no export)? *(Y / N, details)*
- [ ] Can I get real photos of the actual pallets, plus a photo of the labels? *(Y / N, sent by when)*
- [ ] Can I or my inspector see it before I pay? *(Y / N)*

## 7. Closing the call
> "Thanks, **[Name]**. So to confirm: the load is **[available / held until X]**, you hold title, the price is **[$]**, and you'll send the manifest and photos by **[time]**. Is that right? If it checks out, I'll come back to you by **[day]**."

## Pass / fail scorecard
Score the load once the call is done.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Available and not already committed | Pass / Fail |
| Seller can prove title and no lien | Pass / Fail |
| Manifest and real photos received | Pass / Fail |
| Condition and dates acceptable | Pass / Fail |
| No brand-resale, recall or regulatory problem | Pass / Fail |
| Freight + handling still leaves margin | Pass / Fail |
| Seller would sell to me again | Yes / No / Unsure |

**Decision:**
- **Go:** All "must pass" items pass. Quote it to a buyer who has already committed.
- **Hold:** Some items are unanswered. Get the manifest, photos or title proof before doing anything else.
- **Drop:** Any of availability, title, or recall/regulatory checks fails. Don't offer this load to anyone.

## Quick margin check
| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Purchase price | $ |
| + Freight | $ |
| + Handling / storage | $ |
| + Insurance / fees | $ |
| **= All-in cost** | $ |
| Buyer's price (including your 5–10% fee if charged separately) | $ |
| **Gross margin** = (buyer's price − all-in cost) ÷ buyer's price | % |

The board's target was **25% or higher** after all costs.

## Tracking log
This is how you measure your software. Add one row per lead, in a spreadsheet.

| Load ID | Seller | Available? | Title OK? | Manifest + photos? | Price | Go / Hold / Drop | Why dropped |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Three numbers to compute after 10 calls:**
1. **Hit rate:** the share of software leads that were actually available.
2. **Clean rate:** the share that also had clear title and a usable manifest.
3. **Go rate:** the share that passed everything and still had margin.

Anything you can say about accuracy ("X of 10 leads were real and clean") is stronger than a demo.

## Before you use it
- **Legal review:** Ask a lawyer to look at the title and unclaimed-freight rules in your state before your first purchase. Those rules vary, and the sheet only asks the questions.
- **Never pay first:** Don't send a deposit until title and availability are confirmed in writing.
- **Regulated goods:** Food, cosmetics, electronics and batteries have extra rules. Drop them from your first few loads unless you know the requirements.
